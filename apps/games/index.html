How to play

A star is worth 10 points. Finish the round for 50 extra points. Your score also grows as you travel. Use the buttons below the game or tap the game area.

Enter starts or resumes a round. P or Escape pauses. Tab moves between controls. Leaving the window pauses your round; choose Keep playing when you come back.
